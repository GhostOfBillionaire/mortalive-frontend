We believe in calm, authentic connection.

Mortalive was created in 2026 as a response to the overwhelming noise of traditional social media. We wanted a space where people could have real conversations — without pressure, performance, or endless scrolling.

🛡️ Beta status

Mortalive is still being built.

The product is experimental and may change, break, pause, or be discontinued while development continues. This page describes the project at a high level; the legally binding rules for using the service are in the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Our Mission

To rebuild social connection for the modern world — making it safer, calmer, more private, and genuinely human again.

Privacy First

We design features with privacy and user control in mind, while recognizing that no online system can promise absolute security.

Consent Driven

Users are shown the relevant terms and privacy information and, where applicable, must actively accept them before proceeding.

Quality Over Quantity

We optimize for meaningful conversations rather than treating engagement as the only measure of success.

Human at the Core

Technology should support real relationships, not replace them or make promises it cannot keep.

Mortalive is a developing project. Product behavior, infrastructure, policies, and availability may change during the beta period.
