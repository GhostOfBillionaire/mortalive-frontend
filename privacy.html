Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026 · Version 1.0 (Beta)

🛡️ Privacy protection notice

Privacy commitments are specific — not absolute guarantees

Mortalive is a beta-stage service. This policy describes the categories of data the current platform may process and the purposes for which it is used. Security and deletion commitments are made to the extent technically and legally applicable; no internet service can guarantee absolute security or instantaneous deletion in every circumstance.

Where a legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or government-preservation obligation applies, data may be retained for as long as lawfully required even after an account-deletion request.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Mortalive's website and associated services. By using Mortalive, you acknowledge that the platform is in beta and that data processing may evolve as the product and infrastructure change.

2. Information We May Collect

Guest / Session Data

Temporary session identifiers, guest-state information, and technical information necessary to operate a guest session.

Account Data

Username, email address where provided, authentication-related records, password hashes, account identifiers, profile information, scores, badges, links, and other account metadata.

User Content

Profile photos, posts, comments, likes, links, recordings, thumbnails, and other content you choose to upload or create through Mortalive.

Usage & Technical Data

Session activity, feature usage, device and browser details, connection information, IP-related technical information, timestamps, logs, and information necessary for security, diagnostics, abuse prevention, and service operation.

Camera / Microphone & Safety Snapshots

When you grant browser permission, Mortalive may use camera and microphone streams for real-time communication. Certain safety, matching, diagnostics, or verification workflows may also generate temporary still snapshots from video surfaces.

3. Why We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve Mortalive and its features
  • To authenticate accounts and maintain sessions
  • To enable matching, messaging, profiles, feeds, and recordings where applicable
  • To protect users, investigate abuse, and enforce platform rules
  • To diagnose technical issues, prevent fraud, and maintain security
  • To communicate service, security, account, or legal updates
  • To comply with applicable law, legal process, and lawful government requests

4. Data Sharing & Service Providers

Mortalive does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers needed to run the platform, such as hosting, database, authentication, storage, communications, security, or analytics providers, subject to their applicable terms and configurations.

Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users or the public, investigate abuse or security events, or protect Mortalive's rights and infrastructure.

5. Retention & Deletion

Account & User Data

We aim to keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, provide requested functionality, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

Safety Snapshots

The intended retention period for safety snapshots is no longer than 90 days. Actual deletion timing depends on deployed retention controls, backups, storage configuration, security investigations, and any applicable legal preservation requirement.

Account Deletion

Mortalive provides an account-deletion flow where available. The current implementation attempts transactional deletion of user-associated records and the authentication account. Some data may remain where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, audit, or other lawful purposes.

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to a beta-stage service, including encrypted transport where supported by the deployment and password hashing for stored credentials. We also use access controls, authentication checks, rate limits, and other security measures in relevant parts of the platform.

No internet transmission, cloud service, browser, device, database, or security control is guaranteed to be completely secure. You understand that using Mortalive involves residual security risk.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of applicable personal information.
  • You may withdraw permission for camera or microphone access through your browser or device settings.
  • You may stop using the platform or delete your account where the feature is available.
  • Requests may be subject to identity verification, legal exceptions, security requirements, and retention obligations.

8. Grievance & Contact

For privacy, content, safety, or other platform grievances, contact:

Grievance / Support Contact: support@mortalive.com

Use the subject line “Grievance” for a formal complaint so that the request can be routed appropriately. Mortalive will handle grievances within the timeframes applicable to the service and the law governing the relevant complaint.

9. Legal Preservation & Compliance

Nothing in this Privacy Policy prevents Mortalive from preserving or producing information when required or permitted by law, court order, lawful government request, investigation, security incident response, or other valid legal process.

Retention periods may therefore differ from ordinary product deletion periods where the law requires preservation or where preservation is reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service develops, infrastructure changes, or legal requirements evolve. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are published.

This Privacy Policy is effective from August 21, 2026. It should be read together with the Terms of Service.
